About the press

Hi, I'm Ryan.

I make coloring and activity books from Las Cruces, New Mexico. Burrowing Owl Press is named after the little long-legged owls that stand guard out in the desert near home. I like books that are easy to pick up and hard to put down: bold lines you don't have to squint at, puzzles that are fun instead of frustrating, and pictures you'll actually want to finish.
