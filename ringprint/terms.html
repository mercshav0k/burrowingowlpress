Ringprint is made by Burrowing Owl Press in Las Cruces, New Mexico. Questions: ryan@burrowingowlpress.com

What you're buying

A Founding 10 Ringprint is a digital keepsake made from one short voice recording you send us. You get:

  • one full-color artwork file,
  • one printable coloring page, and
  • one reveal video that plays your recording while the art appears.

The price is $29 for the first ten paid orders, then $34. Everything is digital. We don't ship anything physical.

Every Ringprint is one of a kind and made from your recording, so we can't show you the exact result before it's made.

How ordering works

  1. Pay securely through Stripe.
  2. Upload your recording on our order form, using the same email address you paid with.
  3. We review your recording by hand and email you if we need a different clip.
  4. Your private download link arrives by email.

Your recording

  • Voice only for now. Speech, laughter or other voice. No songs or music, including humming along to a song.
  • Length and quality. A clip of about 5–30 seconds with as little background noise as you can. MP3, M4A and WAV work.
  • Permission. You must have any permission needed to use the recording and every voice in it. By sending it, you confirm you do.
  • How we use it. We use your recording only to make and deliver your order. We never use it to train AI or to make other products.
  • Irreplaceable recordings. If this is the only copy of a voice you love, tick the "irreplaceable" box. We review it by hand and never change your original file.
  • What we won't make. We can refuse any recording that is hateful, harassing, sexual, or that we believe you don't have permission to use. If we refuse, you get a full refund.

Turnaround and delivery

We review every order by hand. Your 3 business days start when BOTH are true: your payment is confirmed AND we've accepted your recording as usable. If your clip won't work, we'll email you to ask for another one, and the clock starts when we accept it. If something else holds us up, we'll email you before the deadline.

You'll get a private download link (a Google Drive folder) sent only to your email. Anyone who has that link can open it, so please don't share it. We turn the link off 30 days after delivery, so download your files when they arrive.

One free re-do

If you don't love the result, reply to your delivery email within 14 days. You can send a different clip or ask us to try the same one again. We deliver the re-do within 3 business days of accepting the request (and the new clip, if you send one).

Refunds

  • Before we start work: full refund, just email us.
  • If we can't make a good Ringprint from your clip and a different clip doesn't work either: full refund.
  • If we refuse your recording (see above): full refund.
  • If we miss the 3-business-day promise and you'd rather not wait: full refund.
  • Missing or broken files: tell us and we'll fix or resend them. If we can't, full refund.
  • After a good delivery: because the files are digital and made just for you, we don't refund after delivery. You still get the free re-do.

Refunds go back to your original payment method through Stripe and can take 5–10 business days to appear. Nothing in these terms limits any rights you have under the law.

What you can do with your Ringprint

It's yours for personal use: print it, frame it, share it with family and friends, post it on your own social media. Please don't resell it or use it on products for sale without asking us first.

We keep the copyright in the artwork design and the Ringprint method. We will not post your Ringprint, your recording or your name anywhere (including ads or social media) unless you separately opt in, in writing.

Privacy: what we collect and who handles it

We collect only what we need to make and deliver your order: your name and email (to deliver your files and contact you), your recording and any note you add (to make your Ringprint), and payment confirmation from Stripe. We never see or store your card number.

We share order information only with the services that process and deliver your order:

  • Tally runs the order form and holds your upload until we move it.
  • Stripe processes your payment under its own privacy policy.
  • Google Drive hosts your private download files. Your reveal video contains your original recording.

We don't sell your information. We only email you marketing if you join our list yourself.

How long we keep things

  • Completed orders: we delete your recording and order files (every copy, including the form upload and the download link) 30 days after delivery, or 30 days after your re-do is delivered.
  • Cancelled, refused or refunded orders, and uploads that are never paid for: we delete the recording and any files within 7 days of the cancellation, refusal or refund (or 7 days after an unpaid upload).
  • Sooner? Email us and we'll delete your recording and order files within 7 days and confirm.
  • Longer? If you want us to keep your recording for a future order or gift, ask and we'll confirm in writing.
  • Order record: we keep a short record of each sale (name, email, date, amount, payment reference) with no recording, for as long as bookkeeping and tax rules require.

Age

You must be 18 or older to order. If you're under 18, a parent or guardian needs to place the order.

Changes

If we change these terms, we'll update the date at the top. Orders already placed follow the terms that applied when you ordered.
